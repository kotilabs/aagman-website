// prompt basics

Āagman understands natural language — describe what you want the way you'd explain it to another trader. No forms, no dropdowns. Prompt in whatever language you think in.

Backtest a long-only EMA crossover on RELIANCE, daily, Jan to Dec 2025. Buy when EMA(20) crosses above EMA(50). Stop loss 1.5%, take profit 4.5%. 50 shares per trade.

1. How do I write my first prompt?

Here's the same idea expressed four different ways — one for each thing Āagman does.

  • Backtest: Backtest a long-only EMA crossover on RELIANCE, daily, Jan to Dec 2025. Buy when EMA(20) crosses above EMA(50). Stop loss 1.5%, take profit 4.5%. 50 shares per trade.
  • Screener: Find NIFTY 50 stocks where RSI(14) is above 70.
  • Portfolio: Backtest portfolio of top 3 stocks from RELIANCE, TCS, INFY, HDFCBANK ranked by momentum, monthly rebalance, 10 lakh capital, Jan 2023 to Dec 2024.
  • Live trade: Buy 50 shares of RELIANCE at market, live. Stop-loss 1% below, target 2% above.

Different goals, same principle. Each prompt tells Āagman what instrument, what condition, what risk, and what mode — whether that's a historical backtest, a live order, a stock screen, or a portfolio. What changes across the four is how specific you get with each piece.

2. What are the five pieces of a prompt?

Every Āagman prompt has up to five components. The more complete, the faster you get to results.

PieceWhat Āagman needs to knowExample fragment
SymbolWhich stock, index, or contract — applies to trades, backtests, and screeners alikeRELIANCE / NIFTY 50 universe / BANKNIFTY 24500 CE
Direction & sizeBuy or sell, and how much — for trades and backtests; screeners skip thisBuy 50 shares / Sell 1 lot / Use 10% of capital
Entry ruleThe condition that triggers action — a price, an indicator signal, or a screener filterAt market / When RSI crosses above 30 / ROE above 15%
Exit & riskHow the position closes — for trades and backtests; screeners skip thisStop-loss 2%, target 5% / Trailing stop 3% / Exit after 5 bars
ModeBacktest, live trade, paper trade, or screen / researchLive / Paper / Backtest Jan–Dec 2024 / Screen now

If you skip a piece, Āagman asks you for it rather than guessing. The more complete your prompt, the faster you get to results.

3. What can I ask Āagman to do?

The same prompt structure adapts to live trading, backtesting, portfolios, and screeners.

3.1 Deploy a live or paper trade

This is real execution. Say "live" and Āagman places the order after confirming with you. Say "paper" to simulate. Specify intraday (auto-squares-off at EOD) or delivery/CNC (carries overnight).

Go live: sell 100 SBIN intraday with a 0.5% stop and 1.5% take-profit.
Deploy live: buy NIFTYBEES intraday when RSI(14) crosses above 30, exit when it crosses 70. 1% stop-loss.

Options work the same way — name the structure, strike, expiry, and premium-based exits.

Buy 1 lot of NIFTY 24500 CE at market, live. Stop-loss 30%, target 60%.

For multi-leg strategies, describe each leg with BUY or SELL. Āagman places all legs together and exits them as a unit.

Deploy a live BANKNIFTY short straddle at ATM, 1 lot each leg, combined stop-loss ₹6,000, square-off at 3:15.
Hinglish

Live deploy karo — NIFTY ka RSI 30 ke neeche jaaye toh BankNifty mein bull call spread daal do. Weekly expiry, 1 lot.

3.2 Backtest a strategy

Same structure as a live deploy, but instead of saying "live," give a date range. Āagman runs the strategy historically and returns a performance report.

Backtest a long-only EMA crossover on RELIANCE, daily, Jan to Dec 2025. Go LONG when EMA(20) crosses above EMA(50). Stop loss 1.5%, take profit 4.5%. Buy 50 shares per trade.

You can layer conditions with AND, chain indicators (like EMA of RSI), use z-scores, arithmetic on indicators, or filter by day-of-week — all in plain language.

Buy SBIN when EMA(10) of RSI(14) crosses above 50, daily, Jan to Dec 2024. Exit when it crosses below 45. 100 shares. Stop loss 4%.

Exits go beyond fixed stop/target. Say "trailing stop at 2%" and Āagman tracks the high since entry. Say "exit after 5 bars" for a time stop. Say "use 10% of capital per trade" for percentage-based sizing. Combine them freely.

Backtest a short straddle on NIFTY, Jan to Apr 2025. SELL ATM call and ATM put when INDIA VIX > 20. Monthly expiry. Close when VIX drops below 15.

3.3 Build a portfolio

Define a universe of stocks, tell Āagman how to rank and select, and set a rebalance schedule.

Backtest portfolio of top 3 stocks from RELIANCE, TCS, INFY, HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK, SBIN, WIPRO, LT ranked by momentum score, monthly rebalance, equal weight, 10 lakh capital, Jan 2023 to Dec 2024.

You can rank by momentum, Sortino ratio, returns over a period, or lowest volatility. Weighting can be equal or market-cap based. Add a dynamic filter so only qualifying stocks enter at each rebalance.

...but only include stocks where RSI(14) is below 50 at each rebalance.

Or skip ranking entirely for a buy-and-hold.

Equal weight portfolio of RELIANCE, TCS, INFY, HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK, no rebalancing, buy and hold, 10 lakh capital.

3.4 Run a screener

Screeners are the simplest prompts — just describe what you're filtering for. No date range or sizing needed.

  • Technical: Find NIFTY 50 stocks at their 52-week high.
  • Fundamental: Value stocks with PE below 12, price to book below 1, dividend yield above 4%.
  • Hybrid: ROE > 10 stocks showing bullish MACD crossover. or High margin stocks (operating margin > 25%) near 52-week lows — price < EMA 200.

Use AND for all conditions to match, OR when any one is enough.

4. How do I write better prompts?

SYNTAX

You don't need perfect syntax. "Buy SBIN when EMA 20 crosses EMA 50" works as well as the formal version.

OPTIONS

For options, BUY and SELL mean the option leg itself, not your view on the underlying.

CAPITAL

Starting capital is optional for basic backtests but needed for return percentages or percentage-based sizing.

CONTEXT

A longer prompt rarely breaks anything. A missing piece sometimes does — when in doubt, be explicit.
