A circuit filter halts trading to cool emotions. But the market that reopens is not the same market that paused. Liquidity, spreads, and participant behavior all change, often within the first few minutes.

## What to watch when trading resumes

**Bid-ask spreads** usually widen. Market makers are cautious, and limit order books are thinner. What looked like a liquid stock before the halt can become expensive to exit.

**Volume profile** shifts. The first minute after reopening often sees a spike of trapped traders exiting. After that spike, volume can dry up quickly.

**Price discovery is noisy.** The reopening price may gap through technical levels. Don't assume support and resistance from before the halt still hold.

## A safer approach

Wait for the reopening volatility to settle. The first five minutes are often the most random. If you had a plan before the halt, re-evaluate it against the new order book, not the old chart.

Circuit filters exist to protect you from panic. Don't rush back in before the panic has finished.
