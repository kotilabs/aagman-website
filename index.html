Common questions.

What is āagman?

āagman is an AI trading desk for Indian markets, built by Koti Labs. It hunts for patterns across charts, digs into the data and backtests your ideas, and when you’re ready, takes them live with your broker.

Is āagman a broker?

No. It connects to your existing broker to support execution. It does not custody funds or replace your brokerage.

Does āagman predict markets?

No. It does not attempt to forecast price direction. It helps you structure, test, and maintain your own trading logic under live conditions.

Why can't I just use ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok?

Because they don't operate inside a live market state. They respond to prompts. They don't track evolving positions, ingest real-time feeds as a trading system, compute indicators continuously, or stay synchronized with your execution account.

āagman is connected to live market data, technical structures, and your brokerage environment. It works inside an active trading context, not outside it.

Does āagman trade autonomously with my capital?

No. āagman does not take discretionary control of your money. It connects to your brokerage account to support execution within your defined strategy. You retain control over capital and decision authority.

Is āagman a signal or tip platform?

No. It does not provide stock tips, trade alerts, or copy-trading signals. It supports your own strategy and decision process.

Is āagman regulated by SEBI or NSE?

Yes. āagman is a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (INA000021951) and an NSE-empanelled algo provider under NSE Circular Ref. 40/2026 (3 Jun 2026).

What happens during high volatility?

āagman continues to operate within the parameters you've defined. It does not override your strategy based on emotion or external noise.

Can I use āagman without connecting my broker?

Yes! You can backtest, screen, research, and much more without connecting your broker. The only time you need to connect your broker is when you want to place a live trade.

Can I trade on the same IP address as someone else using the same broker?

No. For example, if you and your friend Rahul are both on the same Wi-Fi network and using the same broker, the broker may flag or restrict both your accounts. To avoid this, make sure each person trades from a separate network or IP address.
